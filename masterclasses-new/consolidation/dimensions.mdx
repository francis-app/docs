Dimensions let you forecast and report below the entity level: by department, channel, project, or any dimension your accounting system tracks. Francis imports them automatically when you sync. This guide takes you from an entity-level breakdown to a working per-dimension model.

## Before you start

Before breaking down by dimension, complete the [consolidation setup](/masterclasses-new/consolidation/consolidation) to apply an entity breakdown. If you only have one entity, you can apply a dimension breakdown directly. On a multi-entity model, applying a dimension breakdown before entity breakdown only affects the first entity. You won't get unified dimensions across entities that way.

## Breakdown by dimension

Breaking down an entity sub-sheet by dimension creates one sub-sheet per dimension value. This is what enables forecasting at the dimension level: by department, channel, or whichever dimension you select.

<Warning>
  Applying a breakdown deletes any pre-existing forecasts and manual entries on the sheet. Do this before you start forecasting, not after.
</Warning>

Hover over the sheet containing the entity actuals, open the action menu (...), then choose **Breakdown** and select your dimension. Francis creates one sub-sheet for each of the first 20 dimension values. If you have more than 20, the remainder are grouped into an **Other** bucket. You can adjust the breakdown afterward to add sub-sheets for the remaining values.

Once applied, the entity sub-sheet becomes a dimension template. Structure (rows, groups, calculations) is defined top-down in the template and syncs automatically to all sub-sheets. Actuals and forecasts are defined bottom-up in the sub-sheets and roll up to the template. You can't edit cell values in the template directly; edit the sub-sheets instead.

## Sub-consolidation by dimension

If you need a consolidated view of a subset of dimension values, open the action menu (...) on the dimension template and choose **Add rollup**. Drag the relevant dimension sub-sheets into it. The rollup shows a consolidated view of those dimension values; the individual sub-sheets remain accessible within it.
