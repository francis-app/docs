Eliminations remove intercompany transactions from the consolidated view. Each elimination is an offset entry on a dedicated Eliminations sheet that references the IC value from an entity sub-sheet and negates it. The entity sub-sheets are not touched. The figures there remain correct for standalone entity reporting.

## Common use cases

### IC revenue and costs

The most common case. One entity bills another for services or goods. Entity A records IC revenue; entity B records the corresponding IC cost. Add one offset row per IC pair on the Eliminations sheet, referencing the IC value from the relevant entity sub-sheet and negating it. Both sides cancel out at group level.

### Management fees

A parent entity charges subsidiaries a management fee. The fee appears as income on the parent and as a cost on each subsidiary. Eliminate both sides. If the fee is a fixed monthly amount, the elimination row can reference a fixed value rather than a GL row.

### IC loans

An intercompany loan appears as a financial asset on the lender's balance sheet and as a liability on the borrower's. Eliminate both on the consolidated balance sheet. Interest on the loan also needs to be eliminated from both the P&L and the balance sheet.

### Multi-currency groups

Because each entity records the same IC transaction in its own base currency, Francis converts each side separately to the reporting currency. The converted amounts often differ slightly due to exchange rate movements. Add an FX fluctuation row to the Eliminations sheet to capture the residual difference, so the two sides net to zero at group level.
