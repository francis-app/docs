Intercompany transactions between entities in your group need to be removed at the consolidated level. Left in, they inflate group revenue, costs, or both. This guide covers how to identify those transactions in Francis and record the eliminations, whether your group operates in a single currency or multiple.

## Before you start

You need a consolidated model with an entity-level breakdown in place before you can record eliminations. If you haven't done that yet, complete the [consolidation setup](/masterclasses-new/consolidation/consolidation) first.

## Applying eliminations

Intercompany transactions are typically tracked through specific GL accounts or dimension values. Isolate them into their own rows on the consolidated sheet first. This makes it straightforward to target them with offset entries without disturbing the rest of the model. Select the tab that matches your group's currency setup.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Same currency">

Add a dedicated sheet (call it "Eliminations") to your consolidation template. For each intercompany transaction, add a row to the Eliminations sheet and use a formula to reference the IC row from the relevant entity sub-sheet, negated. For example, if entity A records IC revenue of 7,300, the Eliminations row references that value from the entity sub-sheet and applies a minus sign. The two entries cancel out at group level. To extend the formula to future months, use Fill right (`⇧ + Tab`). Once filled, those months update automatically as actuals come in.

Keep the intercompany rows intact on the individual entity sheets. Those figures are still correct for standalone entity reporting. Only the group level eliminates them.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Different currencies">

Follow the same steps as for single-currency eliminations. Because the two entities record the same IC transaction in different base currencies, Francis converts each side separately to the reporting currency using its configured exchange rates. The converted amounts will often differ slightly. That difference needs its own row.

Add a row (typically called "FX fluctuation") to your Eliminations sheet and use a formula to calculate the difference between the two converted amounts. For example, entity A records IC revenue of −7,563 and entity B records IC costs of 7,300. The 263 difference goes to the FX fluctuation row, so the two sides net to zero at group level. Use Fill right (`⇧ + Tab`) to extend the formula to future months.

<Warning>
  This approach assumes all residual differences come from exchange rate movements. If the same GL account is used for multiple purposes, or if there are bookkeeping errors, the residual will be overstated and the FX fluctuation row will absorb more than it should.
</Warning>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
