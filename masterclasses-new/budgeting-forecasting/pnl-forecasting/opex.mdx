OPEX covers a wide range of cost lines — software, marketing, facilities, professional services — each with different predictability and drivers. Salaries and payroll are covered on the Headcount page; this page covers the rest.

## Trend-based

The default for most OPEX lines. Project forward from recent actuals using a rolling average to smooth short-term volatility:

```typescript
= AVG_LAST("Software subscriptions", 3)
```

Where seasonal patterns matter, reference the same month last year with a growth adjustment instead:

```typescript
= "Marketing"[-12] * (1+10%)
```

Trend-based works well for stable, recurring costs. It becomes unreliable when a cost line has changed structurally — a renegotiated contract, a shift in spend policy, or a significant scale change.

## Hardcoded

Use hardcoded values for committed costs where the amount and timing are already known — a signed office lease, an annual software contract, a booked consulting engagement. There's no forecasting uncertainty to model; enter the value directly.

## Driver-based

Some OPEX lines have a meaningful driver — marketing spend tied to a percentage of revenue, recruitment costs tied to headcount growth. Where a driver exists and is reliable, use a [formula](/documentation/features/modeling/formulas) to link the cost to its driver.

For most OPEX items, a driver either doesn't exist or isn't reliable enough to justify the complexity. Trend-based is the simpler and often more accurate starting point.
