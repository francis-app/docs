The P&L is the center of gravity in most Francis models. Actuals flow in from your accounting system; the forecast builds forward from the structure you set.

## The Francis P&L template

<Note>Screenshot of the Francis P&L template to be added here.</Note>

Gross margin, EBITDA margin, and net profit margin are pre-built calculations. In the annual total column these show as weighted averages — the correct treatment when margins vary month to month.

Map your GL accounts to the relevant rows before you start forecasting. Actuals won't populate until the mapping is in place.

## Actuals and forecast in one view

Because actuals and forecast live in the same model, you don't need to reconcile a separate file to understand where you stand. When direct costs run over in Q3, you see it immediately — gross margin updates, the full-year total shifts, and the pressure on EBIT is visible in the same view.
