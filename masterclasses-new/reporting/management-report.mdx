# Build a management report

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/masterclasses/reporting/management-report.mp4" />

This template shows a full 20-page management report built in Francis. Each page below shows what it looks like and which settings produce it — so you can replicate any section directly in your own model.

<Update label="Page 1" description="KPI overview">
  ![KPI overview](/images/report/page-01.png)

  **Components shown** — revenue, gross profit, EBITDA, and net income as headline KPIs.
  **Comparison** — actuals vs. budget, current month.
  **Format** — numbers displayed in thousands. Set this in **Settings > Display > Number format**.
  **Date range** — current month, driven by the report date in **Date settings**.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 2" description="Executive summary">
  ![Executive summary](/images/report/page-02.png)

  **Layout** — free-text commentary section alongside a summary table.
  **Commentary** — written directly into a text component. Update each reporting period.
  **Table** — a sheet pulling the top-level P&L rows from your model.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 3" description="Revenue overview">
  ![Revenue overview](/images/report/page-03.png)

  **Rows shown** — total revenue broken down by revenue stream.
  **Columns** — actuals, budget, variance (absolute and %), prior year.
  **Variance colour coding** — enabled in **Settings > Display > Variance colours**.
  **Period** — year to date. Set the YTD toggle in **Date settings**.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 4" description="Revenue by channel">
  ![Revenue by channel](/images/report/page-04.png)

  **Dimensions** — revenue split by channel dimension (retail, wholesale, e-commerce).
  **Chart type** — stacked bar, showing channel mix over the trailing 12 months.
  **Dimension setup** — configure channels in **Settings > Dimensions**.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 5" description="Revenue by geography">
  ![Revenue by geography](/images/report/page-05.png)

  **Dimensions** — revenue split by entity (Denmark, UK, US).
  **Currency** — each entity shown in its local currency, with a group total in reporting currency.
  **FX rates** — pulled automatically from the consolidation settings. See the Consolidation masterclass for setup.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 6" description="Gross profit and margins">
  ![Gross profit and margins](/images/report/page-06.png)

  **Rows shown** — revenue, COGS, and gross profit. Margin % calculated as a formula row.
  **Margin formula** — `"Gross profit" / "Revenue"`, formatted as a percentage.
  **Comparison** — actuals vs. prior year, trailing 12 months.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 7" description="Cost of goods sold">
  ![Cost of goods sold](/images/report/page-07.png)

  **Rows shown** — COGS broken down by cost category (materials, freight, production labour).
  **Driver-based rows** — COGS lines linked to revenue drivers where applicable.
  **Period** — current month and year to date, side by side.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 8" description="EBITDA bridge">
  ![EBITDA bridge](/images/report/page-08.png)

  **Chart type** — waterfall, showing the movement from budget EBITDA to actual EBITDA.
  **Bridge items** — revenue variance, COGS variance, OPEX variance, one-offs.
  **Chart setup** — configure waterfall in the chart component settings panel.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 9" description="Operating expenses overview">
  ![Operating expenses overview](/images/report/page-09.png)

  **Rows shown** — total OPEX broken down by department.
  **Columns** — actuals, budget, variance, prior year.
  **Drill-down** — each department links to its own dashboard page (pages 10–13).
</Update>

<Update label="Page 10" description="Sales and marketing spend">
  ![Sales and marketing spend](/images/report/page-10.png)

  **Rows shown** — headcount costs, software, events, paid media, agency fees.
  **Headcount** — pulled from the headcount model. Ensure headcount is linked to this department.
  **Period** — year to date vs. full-year budget.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 11" description="Product and engineering spend">
  ![Product and engineering spend](/images/report/page-11.png)

  **Rows shown** — headcount costs, contractors, software licences, hosting.
  **Headcount** — engineering and product roles from the headcount model.
  **Period** — current month and year to date.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 12" description="G&A spend">
  ![G&A spend](/images/report/page-12.png)

  **Rows shown** — finance, legal, HR, office, insurance, and other overhead.
  **One-off items** — flagged separately using a dedicated one-off row or tag.
  **Period** — year to date vs. budget.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 13" description="Headcount overview">
  ![Headcount overview](/images/report/page-13.png)

  **Rows shown** — headcount by department, split into employees and contractors.
  **Metrics** — opening headcount, new hires, leavers, closing headcount.
  **Source** — headcount model. Set up roles and departments in the headcount section.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 14" description="Headcount cost breakdown">
  ![Headcount cost breakdown](/images/report/page-14.png)

  **Rows shown** — salary, employer NI/social costs, benefits, bonuses, total cost per department.
  **Employer costs** — modelled as a percentage of salary. Set the rate in headcount settings.
  **Period** — year to date, with a full-year forecast column.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 15" description="Balance sheet overview">
  ![Balance sheet overview](/images/report/page-15.png)

  **Rows shown** — condensed balance sheet: fixed assets, working capital, net debt, equity.
  **Period** — end of current month vs. prior year end.
  **Validation** — balance sheet must balance. Francis flags imbalances automatically.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 16" description="Working capital">
  ![Working capital](/images/report/page-16.png)

  **Rows shown** — trade receivables, trade payables, inventory, prepayments, accruals.
  **Days metrics** — DSO, DPO, DIO calculated as formula rows.
  **Period** — current month end, with a 12-month trailing chart.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 17" description="Cash flow statement">
  ![Cash flow statement](/images/report/page-17.png)

  **Method** — indirect method, derived from P&L and balance sheet movements.
  **Sections** — operating, investing, financing activities.
  **Period** — current month and year to date.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 18" description="Cash and net debt">
  ![Cash and net debt](/images/report/page-18.png)

  **Rows shown** — cash, drawn debt, net debt position, available credit facility.
  **Chart** — net debt waterfall over the trailing 12 months.
  **Covenant tracking** — add covenant metrics as formula rows beneath the main table.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 19" description="Budget vs. actuals — full P&L">
  ![Budget vs. actuals](/images/report/page-19.png)

  **Columns** — actuals, budget, variance (£ and %), prior year, prior year variance.
  **Scope** — full P&L from revenue to net income.
  **Period** — year to date. Switch to current month using the date toggle.
  **Variance colours** — enabled in **Settings > Display > Variance colours**.
</Update>

<Update label="Page 20" description="Rolling forecast">
  ![Rolling forecast](/images/report/page-20.png)

  **Columns** — actuals months (closed), forecast months (open), full-year total.
  **Forecast method** — driver-based for revenue and COGS; departmental budgets for OPEX.
  **Reforecast cadence** — update open months each period by revising the relevant drivers.
  **Lock actuals** — lock closed periods in **Date settings** to prevent edits.
</Update>
