A stacked area chart plots values over time as filled areas stacked on top of one another. Like a stacked column, it shows composition and total per period, but the filled areas emphasize the continuous flow of that composition over time. Lines bounding the areas can be smooth or straight.

## Settings

Click a chart to open its settings, grouped in the sidebar. Remove a group with the **X** next to its name to hide that element from the chart, and use **+** to add a series or a target.

### Chart

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Date range">
    The span of data the chart shows. Choose a dynamic range (Last month, YTD, LTM, Full year, Last year, or Next year) or set a Custom range. Dynamic ranges resolve against the Last close date set for the model, so they roll forward automatically as you close each period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Group by">
    How periods group along the x-axis: monthly, quarterly, calendar year, fiscal year, or all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Colors">
    The color palette applied to the series. Options are Ocean blue, Olive green, Sun burst, Death star, Pastel, Nature, Temperature, Dawn, Blue, Green, Orange, Red, Pink, Purple, and Gray. Override the palette on an individual series from the series settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Grid">
    The gridlines behind the chart: No grid, Horizontal only, Vertical only, or Both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Line style">
    Whether the lines bounding the areas are Smooth or Straight.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Series

The series shown in the chart, each drawn from a row, group, or calculation in your model. For each series you can include multiple sources, such as actuals, budget, or forecast. Series order sets the legend order: the top series shows first, then the next, and so on. Sources are [styled automatically](/features/using-francis/charts/overview#actuals-and-forecast-styling): actuals render in the full series color, and budget or forecast render semi-transparent.

Click a series to reveal its own settings. When a series has a single source, click the series directly. When it has multiple sources, click the specific source to open its settings.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Title">
    The series name. Defaults to the name of the row, group, or calculation the series is drawn from; override it to rename the series.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Color">
    Override the palette color assigned to this series.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Data labels">
    Show or hide the value labels on the series.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Absolute values">
    Plot the absolute value of each point, ignoring sign.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Title

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Content">
    The chart title. Defaults to the name of the row, group, or calculation the chart is drawn from; override it with free text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Align">
    Position the title to the left, center, or right.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Legend

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Position">
    Where the legend sits. Top is the only position available for this chart type.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### X-axis

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Line">
    Show or hide the x-axis line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slant">
    Rotate the x-axis labels by 0, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, or 90 degrees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Y-axis

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Title">
    The y-axis title, as free text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Line">
    Show or hide the y-axis line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Unit">
    Auto inherits the unit from the series in the model, or override it with number, percentage, or a currency (DKK, EUR, USD, or GBP).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Limit">
    By default the axis fits the bounds of the series. Set a fixed minimum and maximum to lock the scale.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Target

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Label">
    The target's name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Value">
    The value the line sits at.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Color">
    The color of the line.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
