The Partner Program is for accountants and fractional CFOs who manage multiple clients in Francis. You create a separate workspace for each client, each with its own access, billing, and data, and get advisor discounts and reusable templates to speed up onboarding. Demo setups with test data are available if you want to showcase Francis.

## One workspace per client

Create a separate Francis workspace for each client. Each workspace can connect to multiple accounting systems, so you can support clients with several legal entities. Switch between client workspaces from the workspace menu in the top left.

## Roles and access

When you and your colleagues join Francis, each of you signs up with your own email address. Once you're a Certified Francis Advisor, your profile is tagged with an Advisor label based on your email domain.

The person who creates a workspace becomes its Owner. To transfer ownership, go to **Settings > Workspace > Members**, open the role menu next to a member's name, and select **Make owner**. A workspace can have multiple owners.

Invite users to any workspace as an Admin, Editor, or Viewer. See [Members and roles](/features/admin/members-and-roles) for what each role can do.

<Note>
  There is no central admin panel across workspaces. Invite each colleague into every client workspace they need access to.
</Note>

To remove someone's access, review each workspace and remove them from the Members page.

## Inviting clients

You can also invite clients into their own workspaces. Pricing depends on the level of access:

- Viewer-only access: 50% discount
- Admin or Editor access: 25% discount

It's currently possible to assign a client Editor rights while using the 50% discount code. This setup is trust-based. Francis reserves the right to review access levels and adjust discounts where they're misaligned, for example if Editor rights are granted under a 50% discount.

## Subscriptions and billing

Each client workspace's subscription is managed separately. You can choose a different plan for each client. See the options at [francis.app/pricing](https://www.francis.app/pricing).

<Note>
  There is no central billing panel, so payments are handled per workspace.
</Note>

You can:

- Pay on behalf of the client by adding your card
- Invite the client to add their own payment details

In both cases, card details are added within the individual workspace's billing settings. Each workspace is invoiced separately, and the invoice includes the client's workspace name for reference.

## Advisor discounts

As a Certified Francis Advisor, you get two discount levels based on how actively the client uses Francis:

- Viewer-only access: 50%
- Editor or Admin access: 25%

The discount reflects client involvement: the more the client uses Francis directly, for example editing budgets, the higher the price. If you use Francis mainly as an internal tool to deliver work, the price is lower.

Once you're a Certified Francis Advisor, you receive two promo codes, one per tier, to apply at checkout.

## Reusing work with templates

One advantage of Francis is reusing models across clients. Use Francis's built-in templates or create your own to share across client workspaces. This captures and scales your best practices. Advisors who build and reuse templates typically get clients running in under an hour.

You can promote any model you've built into a reusable template:

1. Duplicate the model you want to promote. Promoting a model moves it to a new location, so use a copy, not one still in use by a client.
2. Remove client-specific content, such as company names, employee names, and confidential metrics or descriptions.
3. Email [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app) with the URL of the model you want to promote. The URL includes the model ID needed to identify it.

![Model URL](/images/URL.png)

Francis will then:

- Move the model to a dedicated, free workspace called **Template workspace**
- Promote it to a shared template
- Remove any data source mappings automatically

Once promoted, any changes you make to the template are published automatically, so new deployments always use the latest version.

## Demo data

To demo Francis to potential clients, Francis can create a free demo workspace with sample data. Email [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app) to request a demo setup.

Francis will then:

1. Create a new demo workspace under your account.
2. Provide test data for two fictional entities, Bakeries Inc and Webshop Ltd, as Google Sheets ready to import.
3. Offer a short onboarding call to set up a basic model, configure Google Sheets connections, and map the data to a Francis template.

This gives you a ready-to-use environment to showcase Francis without real client data.
