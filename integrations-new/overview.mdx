## How syncing works

### On-demand syncing

Syncing is on-demand. Francis does not auto-sync or run scheduled syncs, but you can sync as often as you want. This applies to every data source you connect, accounting systems, Google Sheets, and currency rates alike.

Trigger a sync from either of two places:

- **Integrations page** — sync each data source individually.
- **Left sidebar** — use the **Data source** card at the bottom to sync all data sources at once.

The Data source card also shows the last sync date. It turns yellow, then red, as that date ages, so a stale connection is easy to spot from the model.

### Global vs delta sync

The first sync is always a global sync: Francis pulls the full history from the connected source, back to its earliest data. This one can take a while.

A delta sync pulls only what's new since the last run, which is much faster. It is currently available for Visma e-conomic and Microsoft Business Central. For those connectors, Francis runs a delta sync after the initial global sync, and falls back to a global sync if it detects major changes to historical data. You don't choose between the two. Francis always routes to the right one.

All other sources run a global sync every time you sync.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Francis support dimensional planning?">
    Dimensional planning is available on Advanced and Mastery plans. Francis automatically detects dimensions in your accounting system, making them available for [breakdowns](/documentation/features/consolidation/breakdowns) in your model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect multiple accounting systems?">
    Yes. You can connect multiple instances from the same or different accounting systems. Use [Mappings](/documentation/features/modeling/mappings) to unify GL accounts across entities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add more connections to my plan?">
    * On the Core plan, upgrade to Advanced or Mastery to add more connections.
    * On the Advanced plan, upgrade to Mastery or add connections to your existing plan.
    * On the Mastery plan, add connections directly to your existing plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Francis write any data back to my systems?">
    No. Francis reads data only. Resolve any data issues at the source before syncing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My sync is stuck at 0%. What should I do?">
    During peak hours, syncs are queued when many workspaces sync at once. The progress bar stays at 0% while queued. If your sync has been stuck for more than 30 minutes, reach out at [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
