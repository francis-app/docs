Use Google Sheets to import data from business tools not natively supported by Francis. By pairing Google Sheets with third-party integrations, you can connect CRMs, data warehouses, or any tool that can export to a spreadsheet.

## Common use cases

| Data type | How Google Sheets is used |
|---|---|
| **Sales data** | Manual or automated input of revenue actuals and customer counts |
| **CRM / Pipeline** | Pull deal and pipeline data from HubSpot, Pipedrive, or similar tools via export or a connector like Weld |
| **Salary / Headcount** | Import employee salary data from payroll or HR tools not connected to Francis |

## Connect Google Sheets

Go to **Settings > Integrations > Google Sheets** and connect your Google account. Francis will create a **Francis Data Source** sheet in your Google Drive. Use this sheet to input your data.

To share the sheet with team members, update its permissions in Google Sheets or move it to a shared drive.

<Tip>
  Francis can only view, edit, create, and delete the specific Google Drive files it creates. It does not access any of your other Google Drive files.
</Tip>

## Data formats

When setting up the connection, choose one of two templates based on how your data is organized.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Monthly totals">
    Use monthly totals if your data is already aggregated by month and does not require drilling down into individual records.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Transactions">
    Use transactions if your data consists of individual records that have not been aggregated into monthly totals. Data imported via this template supports drill-downs in Francis for more detailed analysis.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Microsoft users

If you use Microsoft 365, create a Google account with your Microsoft email address to connect Google Sheets to Francis.

## Syncing

Changes in Google Sheets do not sync automatically. Initiate a sync in Francis to pull the latest data.
