Connect your Visma e-conomic account to import actuals into Francis. Once connected, your chart of accounts is available in the [Mappings](/documentation/features/modeling/mappings) view, where you map e-conomic accounts to line items in your model.

## Connect Visma e-conomic

Go to **Settings > Integrations > Visma e-conomic > Connect**. A prompt will appear asking you to authorize Francis as an application in your Visma e-conomic account. This requires Admin rights.

<Tip>
  Sign in to the right e-conomic company in a separate tab before connecting. The connect flow uses whichever account is signed in, and switching midway can break it. For multiple companies, switch to the right one before each connection.
</Tip>

## What Francis sources

Francis pulls the following data from e-conomic:

- Posted journal entries (bogførte posteringer)
- Draft entries (kassekladder), if enabled
- Dimension tags, available for [breakdowns](/documentation/features/consolidation/breakdowns) within Francis
- GL accounts

## Adjustments

Francis automatically adjusts imported data in three ways. All three rely on the asset range and retained earnings configuration, so it's important that these are set correctly to ensure correct numbers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Number convention">
    Francis flips the sign for income, expenses, liabilities, and equity accounts to follow the Francis sign convention:

    * **Positive (+):** Income (I), Asset (A), Liability (L), and Equity (EQ)
    * **Negative (-):** Expense (EX)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Balance sheet">
    Francis presents balance sheet values as accumulated amounts at a point in time, not as period movements. Imported data is adjusted on the way in to match this convention.

    Summarize balance sheet items using `ENDING` or `DELTA` values instead of `SUM`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Retained earnings">
    When you close a fiscal year, e-conomic moves retained earnings from a "Retained earnings this year" account to a "Retained earnings from previous years" account. Francis supports this by introducing an additional "Retained earnings this year" account.

    If you specified a retained earnings account during setup, this adjustment is applied automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Settings to enable adjustments

Francis relies on two user-set settings to make the adjustments.

### Asset range

If your chart of accounts does not match the default e-conomic template, Francis will prompt you to confirm the asset account range. Adjust `asset_range_start` and `asset_range_end` to match your setup.

<Tip>
  The default asset range for Visma e-conomic is 5000 to 5990. Francis may prompt you to confirm even if your chart follows this default.
</Tip>

### Retained earnings

If your chart of accounts differs from the e-conomic default template, you will also be prompted to specify your retained earnings account.

<Tip>
  In most cases this is account 6130 "Retained earnings last year". To verify in e-conomic, go to **Regnskab > Systemkonti > Årsafslutning**.
</Tip>

## Status checks

Francis runs tests on your imported data to flag setup issues before they distort your numbers.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Missing FX rate on draft journal entries">
    E-conomic has a bug where draft journals (kassekladder) in a foreign currency can be created without an FX rate, even though the system shouldn't allow it. When this happens, Francis cannot process the entry and the sync will fail. To fix it, apply an FX rate to the draft entries missing it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
