Connect your Visma e-conomic account to import actuals into Francis. Once connected, your chart of accounts is available in the [Mappings](/documentation/features/modeling/mappings) view, where you map e-conomic accounts to line items in your model.

## Connect Visma e-conomic

Go to **Settings > Integrations > Visma e-conomic**. After enabling, the e-conomic authorization page will open in a new tab asking you to confirm Francis as an application on your account.

<Tip>
  The authorization page defaults to whichever e-conomic account is currently signed in to your browser. Before starting, sign in to the account you want to connect in a separate tab. To connect multiple e-conomic accounts, repeat the flow once per account, signing in to each one beforehand.
</Tip>

### Draft entries (kassekladder)

To include draft entries (kassekladder or kladdeposteringer), go to **Settings > Integrations > Visma e-conomic** and enable **Include draft entries (kassekladder)**.

Including draft entries lets you analyze the effect of entries on financial reports before closing the books for a month.

### Asset range

If your chart of accounts does not match the default e-conomic template, Francis will prompt you to confirm the asset account range. Adjust `asset_range_start` and `asset_range_end` to match your setup.

<Tip>
  The default asset range for Visma e-conomic is 5000 to 5990. Francis may prompt you to confirm even if your chart follows this default.
</Tip>

### Retained earnings

If your chart of accounts differs from the e-conomic default template, you will also be prompted to specify your retained earnings account.

<Tip>
  In most cases this is account 6130 "Retained earnings last year". To verify in e-conomic, go to **Regnskab > Systemkonti > Årsafslutning**.
</Tip>

### Dimensions

Francis automatically detects the three dimensions in Visma e-conomic, which can be used for [breakdowns](/documentation/features/consolidation/breakdowns) in Francis.

## Adjustments

Francis applies three automatic adjustments to imported data. All three rely on the asset range and retained earnings configuration, so it's important that these are set correctly to ensure correct numbers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Number convention">
    Francis uses a consistent sign convention:

    * **Positive (+):** Income (I), Asset (A), Liability (L), and Equity (EQ)
    * **Negative (-):** Expense (EX)

    Account classification is based on your asset range settings. Verify this configuration before importing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Balance sheet">
    Francis presents balance sheet values as accumulated amounts at a point in time, not as period movements. Imported data is adjusted on the way in to match this convention.

    Summarize balance sheet items using `ENDING` or `DELTA` values instead of `SUM`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Retained earnings">
    When you close a fiscal year, e-conomic moves retained earnings from a "Retained earnings this year" account to a "Retained earnings from previous years" account. Francis supports this by introducing an additional "Retained earnings this year" account.

    If you specified a retained earnings account during setup, this adjustment is applied automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
