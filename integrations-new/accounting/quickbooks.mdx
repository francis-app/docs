Connect your QuickBooks Online account to import actuals into Francis. Once connected, your chart of accounts is available in the [Mappings](/documentation/features/modeling/mappings) view, where you map QuickBooks accounts to line items in your model.

## Connect QuickBooks

Go to **Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks**. Authorize Francis as an application when prompted. Dimensional planning for QuickBooks Online is coming soon.

## Adjustments

Francis adjusts imported data in two ways.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Number convention">
    Francis uses a consistent sign convention:

    * **Positive (+):** Income (I), Asset (A), Liability (L), and Equity (EQ)
    * **Negative (-):** Expense (EX)

    Account classification is based on your asset range settings. Verify this configuration before importing.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Balance sheet">
    Francis presents balance sheet values as accumulated amounts at a point in time, not as period movements. Imported data is adjusted on the way in to match this convention.

    Summarize balance sheet items using `ENDING` or `DELTA` values instead of `SUM`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Retained earnings

Francis does not automatically include a retained earnings account when importing from QuickBooks. Calculate it manually by adding net profit to the opening retained earnings balance:

```typescript
= "Retained earnings"[-1] + "Net profit"[0]
```

<Note>
  Automatic retained earnings for QuickBooks is coming soon. Reach out at [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app) with questions.
</Note>
