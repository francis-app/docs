Your profile is under **Settings > Account > Profile**. It shows your name, email address, and avatar as other members see them. Profile details can't be edited directly. Contact [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app) to make any changes.

## Profile picture

By default, your avatar shows the first and last initials of your name. If you signed up with a Google account that has a public profile picture, Francis uses that image automatically.

## Email address

Your email address is set by the address you used when creating your account. It serves as your unique identifier across every organization you join or create in Francis.

| Action | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| You create an organization using `jane.doe@email.com` | An account is created under `jane.doe@email.com`, and a user is added to that organization. |
| You're invited to join another organization | A user for `jane.doe@email.com` is created in the new organization. |

<Tip>
  If your company is changing domains and you want to update all emails in your organization, contact [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app) from the existing domain and include the new domain in your message.
</Tip>

## Name

Your full name appears to other members whenever they interact with your account. To update it, contact [support@francis.app](mailto:support@francis.app).

## Leave your organization

Go to **Settings > Organization > Members**, select the gear icon next to your name, and choose **Remove from organization**. You lose access immediately. An Owner or Admin would need to re-invite you to restore access.

<Warning>
  If you're the only remaining member in an organization, you can't leave it. To close it, go to **Settings > Organization > General** and select **Delete this organization**. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
</Warning>
