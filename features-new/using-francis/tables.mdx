Tables present model data in dashboards and reports. You build a table from column groups placed side by side, each showing the data a different way: over time, as a variance between versions, or across units in parallel. Tables are created only in dashboards and reports, not in the model view.

## Column groups

A table is one or more column groups arranged left to right. There are three types:

- **Timeline**: a time series shown month over month, quarter over quarter, or year over year.
- **Variance**: a comparison between versions, such as budget vs. actuals, with diff columns.
- **Side-by-side**: entities, departments, or other units compared in parallel columns.

Place as many column groups as you need, of the same or different types, next to each other. For example:

- Monthly columns for the current year, followed by a year-total column. Two timeline groups.
- Actuals month by month for the year to date, followed by actuals vs. budget for the year to date. A timeline group, then a variance group.

### Timeline

A timeline group shows a time series across periods. Its settings sit at the group level:

- **Title**: the column group heading.
- **Date range**: the span of periods to show.
- **Summarize by**: monthly, quarterly, calendar year, or fiscal year.
- **Source**: the version or actuals variant to display. See [Sources](#sources).

The columns are derived from these group-level settings, so you can't edit individual columns in a timeline group.

### Variance

A variance group compares versions. The group itself has two settings:

- **Title**: the column group heading.
- **Date range**: the span of periods to compare.

Within the group you add individual columns of two kinds:

- **Value columns** display values from a source. Settings: **Title** and **Source** (see [Sources](#sources)).
- **Diff columns** show the difference between two value columns. Settings: **Type** (`#`, index, or `%`) and **Baseline** (the value column to measure against).

The first value column is the anchor. A diff measures a specified baseline against that anchor. The anchor is most often actuals. The baseline is most often budget or actuals (last year). 

### Side-by-side

A side-by-side group compares units in parallel columns. Its settings:

- **Date range**: the span of periods to show.
- **Source**: the version or actuals variant to display. See [Sources](#sources).
- **Title**: set per column.

Side-by-side is only available when the table's section uses roll-up sections. Roll-ups tell Francis that all subsheets share the same template and can be compared in parallel columns. You can show either roll-ups or individual sheets. A roll-up functions as a total column, so use one whenever you want a total.

## General settings

These apply to the whole table, across every column group.

- **Section**: the section whose data the table displays.
- **Number scaling**: Default, no scaling, thousands, millions, or billions. Default inherits the model-wide scale set in model settings, reached from the three-dot menu next to the model name in the top-left corner.
- **Decimals**: Auto, or a fixed precision from 0 to 6 decimal places (0, 0.1, 0.12, 0.123, 0.1234, 0.12345, 0.123456). Auto shows no decimals for values above 100, and otherwise targets three significant digits, so 99.532 displays as 99.5 and 4.514 as 4.51. For consistent decimals across a block, set this at the section level.

## Sources

A source is the version or actuals variant a column displays. The list includes every saved version in your model (budgets and forecasts) plus the actuals variants. Actuals and Actuals (last year) are always available.

When the date range extends beyond your last close, covering months you don't yet have actuals for, Actuals + X also becomes available. It shows actuals up to the last closed month, then the version you pick for the months after. Use it to stitch closed actuals to your budget in a single column.
