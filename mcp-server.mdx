## Setup

Two things need to be in place before you can connect an app.

1. **A workspace Owner must authorize the client.** Every client is off by default. An Owner turns each one on separately, under **Settings > AI > Authorized apps**. Once a client is authorized, any member of the workspace can connect it.
2. **You need full access to the workspace.** Members with Limited Viewer access can't connect apps to that workspace. A workspace admin can change your access.
